# Where the Grade 10 and 11 papers come from

Downloaded 2026-10-12 by `fetch_provincial.py` (session scratchpad; its URL list is reproduced below) into
`uploads/provincial/<paper id>/` (git-ignored: no paper content is ever committed). The full machine record, with the
sha256 of every file extracted from a zip, is `uploads/provincial/provenance.json`.

**Licence status: not yet granted.** None of these sites states a licence for reuse in an app. The DBE permission Murci
holds covers the DBE national papers; provincial papers need the province's own permission (or the DBE's, if it covers
them). Until that is on file, `SHOWN_LEVELS` keeps Grade 10 and 11 hidden on the platform.

**Gaps (none published for Grade 10 Nov 2025, searched 2026-10-12: ecexams, NW, KZN via stanmorephysics, testpapers):**
Life Sciences P1, Life Sciences P2, Geography P2. Their stand-ins are the Eastern Cape Nov 2024 papers.
